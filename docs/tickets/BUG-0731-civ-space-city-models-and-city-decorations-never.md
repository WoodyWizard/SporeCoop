## Report
K2b audit of the class BUG-0424: city models and city decorations (worldobj.cpp, BUG-0541) were never checked for content the other game lacks; there is no city ModelKind.

## Root cause
Observed (lab 20261108-033541 and 20261108-034334, content_audit_civ / content_audit_space, both games): every model a placed Civ / Space object draws is a premade or game file: the cities' buildings (city hall 067CEDE1 / 07EDB5B0 of Spore_EP1_Content_02, house 067CEDDC, factory 067C7029, entertainment 067CEDE3) and vehicles (D9F4E98F, FB106D1F, FFE48DC4) of Spore_Content.package, the city decorations (noun 0x18C88E4) and the walls (0x18C7C97) property files of PatchData / Spore_Graphics; none names a part the game lacks, none is unreadable. A city's model is its buildings: they are the civ's designs, which the guest checks with ensure_model (BUG-0469, BUG-0726) and takes from the host when they are the host's own (BUG-0647 vehicles, BUG-0670 buildings, BUG-0725 their lookalike parts). The ornament model 0x2AE5BA7 (+0x228) is no record of any package (generated by the game from its group). The read of a city's three decoration keys at +0x528 (docs/re/space.md#space-city-ornaments) gave pointer-like values on the Civ fixture (noted there, to check).

## Fix
None needed: no city content outside the design keys already checked. The audit stays as a test.

no-fail-run: nothing to fix; evidence out/lab/20261108-033541, out/lab/20261108-034334 (content_audit_civ, content_audit_space OK).
