## Report
Owner: "Titan couldn't get close to a creature. Upon walking up to them, the other creatures would walk away at the exact same speed, or pushed away. So they couldn't attack or impress."

## Root cause
Observed live 2026-11-16 12:14-12:27 (62-dev, the owner hosting Dorger.spo on TITAN's Steam Spore, QA guests Titan (TITAN instance 8, its own creature 'Slank', 7C068C08) and MSI ('Yankin', 7CE63A2C); read-only console and memory reads of the host): Titan's puppet on the host (20634230) was NOT in the mod's puppet list (game.cpp g_puppets held only MSI's 20687738), so on the host it was an ordinary creature of the world, not a partner. Consequences seen: creature_hp's party species list (creature.relations) held MSI's species only (g_party_species = {20CBAEB0}, Titan's 3153CEC0 never), and the herbivore herds near Titan (GamerToes 291EE54D, Slank 29232BF3) backed away from it on the host: they started moving when Titan was 6-8 m away (centre to centre, before any contact), in Titan's direction of travel, at 1-2.5 m/s, playing animation 026F1F86 (not the walk 04866DAB / 04866D8E) and stood again once it stopped: the owner's "they're being pushed, not turning around". MSI walked into the same GamerToes (0.6 m) and the same Slank herd (0.3 m) and nothing moved. Not the size (radius 1.60 on both games), not a stimulus (the GamerToes' stimuli named only MSI's puppet), not the sprint (+0xBB0 0), not modes.cpp creature_alive (g_puppets_gone_alive 0). Inferred: with the puppet unregistered, the herbivores' AI sees a creature of another species (the game's diet / size rule, docs/re/creature.md#creature-relations) and keeps away; the owner's revive hint fits as the moment the puppet changed (not proven). Not known yet: which step took Titan's puppet off the list between its spawn (12:12:13, registered) and 12:15.
CORRECTION (2026-11-16 13:31, feat/lineages-47): the missing list entry is NOT the cause. After both guests respawned (13:30) both new puppets were in the host's list and the party species held both (Titan 3153CEC0, MSI 20CBAEB0), and the GamerToes still backed away from Titan (from 3.1 m, 2.5 m/s, animation 026F1F86) while MSI walked up to 0.4 m with no reaction.
