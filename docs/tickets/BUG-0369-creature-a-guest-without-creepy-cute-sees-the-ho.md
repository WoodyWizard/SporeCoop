## Report
Host mark (Creature, after the host's editor): "My friend sees my creatures as cubes and different
color after using DLC colors and parts." The guest is a Steam game without Creepy & Cute
(`creations: content: Creepy & Cute parts pack NOT present`); its log shows the host's creature's 10
pack parts shown as base parts (BUG-0354) and nothing about paints.

## Root cause
Observed: a creature model names its paint layers by skin effect ids (header bytes 26..37). The pack's
48 paints use 36 effects only the pack has (its compiled effect directories); BUG-0354 substituted
pack parts but sent the pack's paint effects unchanged. On lane 10 (both games without the pack, the
host's Tiger Horse with the pack's Dottie base and coat) the guest drew the host's creature black.
Not reproduced: "cubes" (see docs/re/creations.md#creations-paint-substitutes).

## Fix
`creations.paint_substitute` (creations.cpp `substitute_paints`, next to the part substitutes, only on
a game without the pack): each pack effect in this game's copy of the model becomes the base effect of
the same layer with the nearest default colour (content_packs.h `kCreepyCutePaintSubstitutes`,
generated by tools/paint_substitutes.py), in place. The model's colours stay. `coop.creations paints
<key>` (tests).
